Data Science for Geographers

Interactive illustrations

These go alongside the practical notebooks. Each one lets you play around with one of the ideas from a practical, using the same (simulated) data. There will be more of these as we go through the course!

  • Practical 1

    The join explorer

    What does each type of join actually do to our data? And why did we use a left join to count the tobacco retailers in each datazone?
  • Practical 2

    The confounding explorer

    Why did the effect of tobacco retailers on smoking rates get so much smaller when we added deprivation to our model? And what does "adjusting" for something actually mean?
  • Practical 2

    The outlier explorer

    We removed six datazones with extremely high numbers of tobacco retailers before running our models. How much difference did that decision actually make?
  • Practical 2

    The correlated predictors explorer

    Only income deprivation was associated with tobacco retailers when we put all seven domains of deprivation into our model. So do the other kinds of deprivation not matter?
  • Practical 3

    The residuals explorer

    Our "bad" data had two things wrong with it at once. What does each problem do to the residual plots on its own? And can we always tell that something is wrong?
  • Practical 4

    The reference category explorer

    Why did we get four coefficients for five SIMD quintiles? What happens if we pick a different reference category? And why not just treat the quintiles as numbers?
  • Practical 4

    The coin flip explorer

    How likely is each number of tails when we flip a coin 100 times? And what does this tell us about smoking rates in datazones with only a few pregnant women?
  • Practical 4

    The logistic regression explorer

    Why doesn't a straight line work for a yes/no outcome? What are odds and log-odds, and why does logistic regression use them? And what happens if we move our 20% cut-off?
  • Practical 4

    The counts and overdispersion explorer

    Why does a Poisson regression need an offset? And what actually goes wrong when our counts are overdispersed?
  • Practical 6

    The neighbours explorer

    Which datazones count as neighbours? What difference do queen, rook and nearest neighbours make, and what do spatial weights and the spatial and temporal lags actually do?
  • Practical 6

    The difference-in-differences builder

    Did the licence cap work? Build the estimate one step at a time, and see what goes wrong when the trends aren't parallel.
  • Practical 6

    The interactions explorer

    What does treated * post do that treated + post doesn't? See which coefficients add up to each prediction, and what an interaction between retailers and urban/rural means.
  • Practical 6

    The autocorrelation explorer

    Is the pattern on the map more than chance? Shuffle the map (and the years) yourself and see how Moran's I and its permutation test work.
